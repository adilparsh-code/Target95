# Class XII Boolean Algebra Learning Standard

Boolean Algebra is treated as a flagship concept-first chapter.

Students learn notation, truth tables, gates, laws, simplification, minterms/maxterms, SOP/POS and circuit thinking as one connected system.

The page includes plain-language terminology, complete truth tables, a repeatable construction method, law intuition, worked derivations, truth-table challenges, MCQs, output tracing, debugging, graded practice, exam strategy and mastery checks.

Quality rule: every identity should be understandable from its logic meaning, a truth table, or a named Boolean law.
