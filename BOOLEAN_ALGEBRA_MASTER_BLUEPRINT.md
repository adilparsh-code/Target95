## AI TUTOR SPECIFICATION
### FREQUENTLY ASKED DOUBTS
1. "Why do De Morgan's laws work the way they do?"
2. "How do I correctly group variables in K-Maps?"
3. "What's the difference between SOP and POS forms?"
4. "When should I use K-Maps vs algebraic simplification?"
5. "What are don't-care conditions and how do I use them?"
6. "How do I convert a word problem into a Boolean expression?"
7. "Why are NAND and NOR gates called universal gates?"
8. "What's the difference between XOR and OR operations?"

### COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS
1. Misconception: "Boolean addition is the same as regular addition"
2. Misconception: "K-Maps can only group powers of 2 in square shapes"
3. Misconception: "De Morgan's laws apply to only two variables"
4. Misconception: "All logic gates can only have two inputs"
5. Misconception: "SOP and POS are mutually exclusive forms"

### TYPICAL MISTAKES (AI Tutor to proactively identify)
1. Incorrect application of De Morgan's law (failing to invert both operations and variables)
2. K-Map grouping errors (non-power-of-2 groups, crossing invalid boundaries)
3. Truth table construction mistakes (missing input combinations)
4. Distributive law misapplication (trying to distribute OR over AND incorrectly)
5. Forgetting that 1+1=1 in Boolean algebra (trying to carry over like regular addition)

### HINT STRATEGY
- **Beginner students:** Provide 3 progressive hints for each problem type
- **Intermediate students:** Provide 1 targeted hint for the specific step they're stuck on
- **Advanced students:** Provide only directional guidance to encourage self-solution
- **Universal hint sequence:** 1. Recall relevant law → 2. Identify the operation type → 3. Walk through step-by-step

### EXPLANATION STRATEGY
- **Multimodal explanations:** Combine text description + diagram + code example for every concept
- **Real-world analogies:** Use traffic lights, voting systems, and household circuits to explain abstract logic
- **Progressive complexity:** Start with 2-variable examples before moving to 3-4 variable problems
- **Error-specific remediation:** When a student makes a mistake, generate a custom explanation addressing their exact error, not generic content
- **Spaced repetition integration:** Revisit core concepts in subsequent AI tutor sessions to reinforce long-term retention
