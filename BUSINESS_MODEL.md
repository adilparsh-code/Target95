## 💼 Business Model Overview

Target95+ operates on a **freemium SaaS model** with multiple revenue streams, targeting students, parents, teachers, and schools.

### Revenue Streams

```
1. B2C (Business to Consumer) - 60% revenue
   - Premium subscriptions (students)
   - In-app purchases
   - Certification fees

2. B2B (Business to Business) - 35% revenue
   - School partnerships
   - Teacher subscriptions
   - Coaching institute partnerships

3. B2B2C (Business to Business to Consumer) - 5% revenue
   - White-label solutions
   - API licensing
   - Content licensing
```

### Business Model Canvas

```
Key Partners:
  - Schools & coaching institutes
  - Teachers & tutors
  - Content creators
  - Technology providers (AI, cloud)

Key Activities:
  - Content creation
  - Platform development
  - AI/ML research
  - Customer support

Key Resources:
  - Technology platform
  - Content library
  - AI models
  - Brand & reputation

Value Propositions:
  - Personalized learning
  - AI-powered tutoring
  - Board-specific content
  - Affordable pricing
  - Proven results (95+ scores)

Customer Relationships:
  - Self-service (freemium)
  - Automated (AI tutor)
  - Personal assistance (premium)
  - Community (forums, groups)

Customer Segments:
  - Students (Class 10-12)
  - Parents
  - Teachers
  - Schools
  - Coaching institutes

Channels:
  - Mobile apps (iOS, Android)
  - Web platform
  - App stores
  - Social media
  - Schools
  - Word of mouth

Cost Structure:
  - Technology (30%)
  - Content (25%)
  - AI/ML (20%)
  - Marketing (15%)
  - Operations (10%)

Revenue Streams:
  - Premium subscriptions (60%)
  - School partnerships (25%)
  - Teacher subscriptions (10%)
  - Other (5%)
```

3. Win-Win-Win
   - Students get better education
   - Parents see results
   - Teachers earn income

   - We build sustainable business

## 📈 Growth Strategy

### Growth Phases

```
Phase 1: 1K → 10K students (v1.0 - v1.1)
  Timeline: 6 months (Jan - Jul 2025)
  Strategy: Product-led growth
  Channels: SEO, content marketing, referrals

Phase 2: 10K → 50K students (v1.2 - v2.0)
  Timeline: 6 months (Jul 2025 - Jan 2026)
  Strategy: Multi-channel growth
  Channels: SEO, schools, teachers, paid ads

Phase 3: 50K → 100K students (v2.0 - v3.0)
  Timeline: 6 months (Jan 2026 - Jul 2026)
  Strategy: Dominance & expansion
  Channels: Schools, partnerships, brand
```

```

Tactics:
  - Generous free tier (50 questions/day)

  - Frictionless onboarding
  - Quick wins (first success in 5 minutes)

Metrics:

```
