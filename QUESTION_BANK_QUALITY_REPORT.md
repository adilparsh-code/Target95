### PYQ Mapping Requirements (Per CISCE Blueprint)
All PYQs must be classified by marks, aligned with current curriculum, and tagged with year of appearance:
- 1 Mark: Factual recall, basic definitions, MCQs
- 2 Marks: Short explanations, proof of basic laws
- 3 Marks: Medium complexity problems, code writing
- 4 Marks: Advanced simplification, full program development
- Programming Questions: Only in practical exams (10 marks each)
