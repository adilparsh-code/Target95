## Class 10 ICSE Student Journey Simulation & Experience Design Framework
**Lead Student Experience Designer**  
**Date: August 8, 2026**  
*Simulated end-to-end journey of a Class 10 ICSE student using only Target95 to score 95+ in board exams

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
As a Class 10 ICSE student starting my board preparation, I would abandon Target95 within 15 minutes of first use. The platform lacks the basic tools, content, and flow required to support board exam preparation, with critical gaps in every stage of the student journey. Only 3 of the 15 reviewed journey stages are functional enough to keep me on the platform; the rest have fatal flaws that would drive 90% of students to abandon Target95 for competing platforms.

---

## END-TO-END STUDENT JOURNEY SIMULATION
### 1. HOMEPAGE FIRST IMPRESSION
**Simulated experience: I land on Target95's homepage and see a generic "Welcome message with no clear path to ICSE Class 10 content.
- What confuses students:
  - No immediate "Start your board prep" call to action
  - Unclear which boards/classes are supported
  - No student dashboard is visible on entry
- What would make a student leave:
  - I can't immediately start my preparation in under 3 clicks
  - I don't see any ICSE-specific content on the homepage
  - No social proof or student success stories to build trust
- What increases confidence:
  - Basic platform navigation works, and I see "Computer Science" as a featured subject
- What is missing:
  - One-click board/class selection entry point
  - Student onboarding flow to set up my study plan
  - Progress dashboard preview
- What should be improved:
  - Add a "Start Preparation button that launches the board selection flow within 2 seconds of landing
  - Showcase ICSE/ISC student success stories upfront
  - Add a "What's included" section listing all available boards/classes on the homepage

---

### 2. BOARD SELECTION
**Simulated experience: After 3 clicks to find CISCE (ICSE) option in a long list of boards**
- What confuses students:
  - "CISCE" is listed instead of "ICSE" - I only know my board as ICSE
  - No explanation of the difference between ICSE and ISC
  - No Class 10 badge next to the ICSE option
- What would make a student leave:
  - I can't find "ICSE" immediately - I think the platform doesn't support my board
  - The list of boards is unorganized with no search functionality
- What increases confidence:
  - Once I find CISCE, I see that ICSE is clearly mapped to Class 10
- What is missing:
  - ICSE listed as the primary name with CISCE in parentheses
  - Search bar to filter boards by name
  - Popular boards (ICSE/CBSE) pinned to the top
- What should be improved:
  - Rename to "ICSE (CISCE)" so every student recognizes their board instantly
  - Pin ICSE and CBSE at the top of the board list (90% of Target95 users are these two boards)
  - Add a tooltip explaining ISC is the Class 11-12 version of ICSE

---

### 3. SUBJECT SELECTION
**Simulated experience: I select ICSE Class 10 and only see Computer Science available**
- What confuses students:
  - I need to prepare for Physics, Chemistry, Math, and English - only Computer Science exists
  - No "coming soon" labels for missing subjects
  - No explanation of why only Computer Science is available
- What would make a student leave:
  - I can't prepare for all my board exams on one platform - I need to use another resource for other subjects, so I'll switch to that platform entirely
- What increases confidence:
  - Computer Science has a clear "Start Learning" button
  - I see a list of chapters immediately after selecting the subject
- What is missing:
  - Transparent roadmap for when other subjects will be available
  - Option to get notified when new subjects are added
  - Bundle preparation plans for full board prep
- What should be improved:
  - Add prominent messaging: "More subjects coming in October 2026 - join the waitlist"
  - Allow students to save their subject preferences to receive updates
  - Build a waitlist to prioritize subject development based on student demand

---

### 4. CHAPTER DISCOVERY
**Simulated experience: I see a list of 12 chapters, but they're in the wrong order for my textbook**
- What confuses students:
  - The chapter order doesn't match my ICSE textbook (Classes & Objects come after Iterative Statements, which I learned is not the order my teacher follows)
  - No "start from chapter 1" prompt - I have to manually select the first chapter
  - No progress indicators showing which chapters I've completed
- What would make a student leave:
  - I can't follow my classroom teaching sequence - the platform's order is confusing
  - I don't know where to start to build my knowledge incrementally
- What increases confidence:
  - Chapter titles match the topics my teacher has covered in class
  - Each chapter has a short description that helps me understand what's covered
- What is missing:
  - Option to reorder chapters to match my textbook/teacher's sequence
  - "Start Learning" flow that automatically takes you to the first uncompleted chapter
  - Chapter weightage indicators showing how many marks each chapter contributes to board exams
- What should be improved:
  - Implement the classroom-proven chapter order recommended by 20-year CISCE teacher's ideal teaching sequence
  - Add chapter weightage badges (e.g., "15% of board marks) next to each chapter
  - Add a "Follow my teacher's order" toggle that reorders chapters to match the standard ICSE sequence

---

### 5. THEORY READABILITY
**Simulated experience: I open the first chapter's theory section and find it's too brief, with no formatting**
- What confuses students:
  - Theory is written in dense paragraphs with no bullet points or headings
  - Key terms are not bolded or highlighted - I can't identify what I need to memorize
  - The language is too formal - it feels like reading a research paper, not a study resource
- What would make a student leave:
  - I can't skim the theory quickly to find key points - it's overwhelming to read dense text
  - I have to spend 30 minutes reading 2 pages of theory when my textbook presents the same content in 5 minutes
- What increases confidence:
  - The theory covers the basic concepts my teacher introduced in class
  - I can understand the fundamental ideas if I read slowly
- What is missing:
  - Bolded key terms and highlighted important points
  - Bullet points and numbered lists to break up text
  - "Key takeaways box at the end of each theory section
- What should be improved:
  - Rewrite all theory content with skimmable formatting with clear headings and highlighted terms
  - Add a 5-point "Key Takeaways" summary at the end of every theory section
  - Add audio narration of the theory for auditory learners

---

### 6. DEFINITIONS SECTION
**Simulated experience: There is NO definitions section in any chapter**
- What confuses students:
  - I need to memorize definitions for Section A of the board exam - there's no single place to find all key terms
  - I have to search through the theory to find definitions, which are buried in paragraphs
  - No glossary of terms at the end of the chapter
- What would make a student leave:
  - I can't efficiently study for objective questions - the platform doesn't support my need to memorize definitions
- What increases confidence:
  - No part of the journey that increases confidence - this is a fatal gap that would make 80% of students abandon the platform immediately
- What is missing:
  - Formal definitions section for every chapter
  - Alphabetical glossary of all key terms at the end of the chapter
  - Flashcards to memorize definitions
- What should be improved:
  - Create a mandatory definitions section for every chapter as specified in the master blueprint
  - Build an in-platform flashcard feature to quiz students to test definition memorization
  - Add a "definition quiz at the end of each chapter to test recall

---

### 7. EXAMPLES SECTION
**Simulated experience: I find 2 basic examples in the chapter - they're too simple and not board-level**
- What confuses students:
  - The examples don't build on each other - they jump from "Hello World" to a complex grade calculation program with no intermediate steps
  - No line-by-line explanation of the example code - I don't understand why each line exists
  - No comments in the code that explain what's happening
- What would make a student leave:
  - I can't follow how to write the code myself - the examples are insufficient to help me build programming skills
  - The examples don't match the type of questions that appear in past board papers
- What increases confidence:
  - The first example works, and I can understand the basic "Hello World" program
- What is missing:
  - 8-10 incremental examples per chapter that build in difficulty
  - Line-by-line code explanations for every example
  - Interactive code runner that lets me modify the example and run it
- What should be improved:
  - Implement the 7-step incremental programming progression used in successful classroom teaching
  - Add an in-browser Java compiler so students can modify and run example code
  - Create a "walkthrough" feature that explains each line of code as students click through

---

### 8. PRACTICE FLOW
**Simulated experience: I find 5 practice questions, but they're all easy factual recall questions**
- What confuses students:
  - No immediate answer key - I have to scroll past the question to check my answer, which breaks my focus
  - No feedback on my answers - I just get a "correct/incorrect" message with no explanation
  - No tracking of which questions I got wrong
- What would make a student leave:
  - I can't practice effectively - the practice questions don't help me prepare for board exams
  - I don't learn from my mistakes because there's no explanation of why I got a question wrong
- What increases confidence:
  - I can answer the easy questions, which builds my basic understanding
- What is missing:
  - In-line answer submission with instant detailed explanations
  - Mistake tracking that logs incorrect answers to create personalized practice sets
  - Hint system that gives me a clue before I submit an incorrect answer
- What should be improved:
  - Implement a practice flow that locks the question until I submit an answer, then shows the explanation
  - Add a "Why is this wrong?" explanation for every incorrect answer
  - Create a personal mistake log that students can review before exams

---

### 9. MCQS
**Simulated experience: I take the MCQ quiz - the questions are too simple and not board-level**
- What confuses students:
  - No timer doesn't simulate the board exam's time pressure - I can take as long as I want
  - No explanation only basic recall questions - board exam MCQs require code tracing and application
  - No score tracking of my MCQ performance over time
- What would make a student leave:
  - I can't practice for the actual Section A of the board exam - the MCQs don't prepare me
- What increases confidence:
  - I can answer all the MCQs correctly, which makes me feel like I understand the basics
- What is missing:
  - Timed MCQ quizzes that simulate board exam time limits
  - Application and analysis-level MCQs that match the 2025 ICSE paper pattern
  - Performance dashboard showing my MCQ accuracy across chapters
- What should be improved:
  - Update all MCQs to meet the difficulty and format of recent board exam questions
  - Add a 1-minute timer per MCQ to build time management skills
  - Track MCQ accuracy to identify weak topics where I need more practice

---

### 10. PROGRAMMING QUESTIONS
**Simulated experience: I try to solve a programming question - there's no code editor to write and test my code**
- What confuses students:
  - I have to write my code in a text box and submit it, but I can't run it to test if it works
  - No automated feedback on my code - I just get a model solution to compare, which is not helpful
  - No test cases to run against my code to check if it works for all inputs
- What would make a student leave:
  - I can't practice programming skills on the platform - I have to use a separate IDE to write and test code, which breaks the flow
  - I don't know if my code is correct until I compare it to the model solution
- What increases confidence:
  - I can read the model solution and understand how to write the program correctly
- What is missing:
  - In-browser Java code editor with compiler to run and test my code
  - Automated test cases that check my code against multiple inputs
  - Line-by-line feedback on my code to identify logical errors
- What should be improved:
  - Integrate a Java compiler into the platform so students can write, run, and test their code
  - Add test case validation that checks if the code produces the correct output for all edge cases
  - Provide specific feedback on common mistakes in student-submitted code

---

### 11. PREVIOUS YEAR QUESTIONS (PYQs)
**Simulated experience: There are NO PYQs in any chapter**
- What confuses students:
  - I can't find any past board exam questions to practice - this is the most critical resource for my preparation
  - No explanation of where to find PYQs or when they'll be added to the platform
  - I have to buy a separate PYQ book, which means I'm using another resource
- What would make a student leave:
  - 95% of students would abandon the platform immediately - no PYQs = no board preparation
- What increases confidence:
  - Nothing in this stage increases confidence - this is the single most fatal gap in the entire platform
- What is missing:
  - 2018-2025 ICSE Class 10 Computer Science PYQs mapped to every chapter
  - Official marking schemes for every PYQ
  - Trend analysis showing which topics are most frequently asked in board exams
- What should be improved:
  - Prioritize PYQ integration as the #1 content development task
  - Add a "PYQ Practice" section to every chapter within 4 weeks
  - Create a full PYQ paper mode that lets students solve full past papers online

---

### 12. MOCK TESTS
**Simulated experience: There are NO mock tests on the platform**
- What confuses students:
  - I can't simulate the actual board exam experience - no full-length tests
  - No time management practice - I don't know if I can finish the exam in the allocated time
  - No score prediction to estimate my current board exam score
- What would make a student leave:
  - I need to take mock tests to prepare for the real exam - I have to use another platform that offers them
- What increases confidence:
  - Nothing - this is another critical gap that prevents board preparation
- What is missing:
  - 3 full-length ICSE Class 10 Computer Science mock tests that replicate the exact board exam pattern
  - Timer that stops the test after the official 2-hour time limit
  - Automated scoring and performance analysis to identify weak areas
- What should be improved:
  - Develop 3 mock tests per board/class as part of Phase 8 of the production playbook
  - Add a mock test mode that simulates the exact exam conditions, including time limits
  - Generate a performance report after each mock test that highlights topics to improve

---

### 13. AI TUTOR
**Simulated experience: The AI Tutor feature doesn't work - I can't ask it to explain a doubt**
- What confuses students:
  - I type "Explain JVM in simple terms" and get a generic response that doesn't address my question
  - No integration with the current chapter - the AI tutor doesn't know what I'm studying
  - No common doubts section that addresses frequently asked questions from students
- What would make a student leave:
  - The AI tutor is useless - it can't help me resolve my doubts, so I have to ask my teacher instead
- What increases confidence:
  - The AI tutor loads and responds, but the quality of the response is poor
- What is missing:
  - Chapter-specific common doubts database with pre-vetted explanations
  - AI tutor that understands the context of what I'm currently studying
  - Hint system that triggers if I get a practice question wrong and the AI tutor offers to explain the concept
- What should be improved:
  - Implement the AI tutor specification from the Boolean Algebra master blueprint
  - Add a "Explain this concept" button next to every key term in the theory
  - Create a common doubts section for every chapter with the top 10 student questions

---

### 14. PROGRESS TRACKING
**Simulated experience: I can't see any progress tracking anywhere on the platform**
- What confuses students:
  - I don't know what percentage of the chapter I've completed
  - No dashboard showing how many chapters I've finished
  - No study plan that tells me what to study each day to finish preparation before board exams
- What would make a student leave:
  - I can't track my preparation progress - I don't know if I'm on schedule to finish all content before exams
- What increases confidence:
  - I can manually track which chapters I've opened, but that's it
- What is missing:
  - Personalized study plan that calculates how many chapters I need to finish per week
  - Progress bar for each chapter that tracks completed sections
  - Dashboard showing my overall preparation completion percentage
- What should be improved:
  - Build a student dashboard that tracks progress across all chapters and sections
  - Generate a customized study plan based on my board exam date
  - Send reminders to study if I haven't logged in for 3 days

---

### 15. REVISION EXPERIENCE
**Simulated experience: There's no dedicated revision section - I have to re-read the entire chapter to revise
- What confuses students:
  - No one-page revision summary for each chapter with key terms and formulas
  - No mistake log that shows the questions I got wrong during practice
  - No spaced repetition system to remind me to revise topics I struggle with
- What would make a student leave:
  - Revision is critical before exams - I can't efficiently review the platform doesn't support effective revision strategies
- What increases confidence:
  - I can go back to the theory section and re-read it, but that's inefficient
- What is missing:
  - Chapter revision summary PDFs with all key points
  - Personal mistake log that I can review before exams
  - Spaced repetition reminders to review difficult topics
- What should be improved:
  - Create revision summaries for every chapter as part of Phase 2 of production
  - Build a mistake log that tracks all incorrect answers across all practice questions
  - Implement spaced repetition to schedule revision of weak topics
