# Class XII Boolean Algebra — Teaching Design

Boolean Algebra is treated as a flagship chapter, not a conventional notes page.

## Student experience

1. **Why it exists** — connect 0/1 logic with switches, gates and computing.
2. **Mental models** — understand what each law means before memorising notation.
3. **Law deck** — learn laws as reusable patterns.
4. **Worked proofs** — every meaningful rewrite names the law used.
5. **Truth-table verification** — use tables to discover/check identities.
6. **Visual simplification patterns** — absorption, complements and De Morgan get memorable explanations.
7. **Three difficulty levels** — foundation, exam and advanced reasoning.
8. **Error clinic** — students diagnose incorrect Boolean transformations.
9. **Practice ladder** — easy → medium → hard, with revealable solutions.
10. **Exam mode** — speed strategy, law selection and proof-writing guidance.

## Design principle

The student should be able to answer both:

- “What is the rule?”
- “Why does the rule work?”

A chapter is not considered complete merely because the Boolean laws are listed.
