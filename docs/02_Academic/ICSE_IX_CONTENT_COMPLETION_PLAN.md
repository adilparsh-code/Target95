## Required content contract for every chapter

Every chapter must contain:

1. Syllabus mapping and learning objectives
2. Student-friendly theory
3. Definitions and key terms
4. Important syntax/rules/formulae where applicable
5. Worked programming/examples
6. Common mistakes and misconceptions
7. Exam tips
8. At least 10 chapter practice questions
9. Programming questions where the topic is programming-oriented
10. Output-tracing questions where applicable

13. Practical/lab linkage where applicable
