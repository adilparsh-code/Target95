# ISC AI 883 — Board Practice Set 01

## Class XI

| Q | Type | Marks | Difficulty | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | MCQ | 1 | Easy | Which AI capability is most directly involved when a system interprets human language? | NLP / language processing |
| 2 | MCQ | 1 | Easy | Which measure identifies the most frequently occurring value? | Mode |
| 3 | Short | 2 | Easy | State two uses of data visualization. | To communicate patterns/trends and support comparison or interpretation of data. |
| 4 | Short | 3 | Medium | Explain why data cleaning is important before modelling. | It reduces problems caused by missing, inconsistent or erroneous data and improves the reliability of subsequent analysis/model training. |
| 5 | Numerical | 3 | Medium | Find the mean of 6, 8, 10, 11, 15. | 10 |
| 6 | Application | 4 | Medium | A school plots monthly attendance using a line graph. Why is a line graph appropriate? | It shows change/trend over an ordered time sequence, making month-to-month movement easy to observe. |
| 7 | HOTS | 4 | Hard | A model predicts exam performance using study hours. Explain one reason why a strong correlation should not automatically be interpreted as causation. | Other variables may influence performance; correlation shows association in observed data, not proof that one variable causes the other. |
| 8 | Case Based | 5 | Hard | An AI system trained mostly on data from one demographic performs poorly for another. Identify the ethical concern and suggest safeguards. | Bias/fairness concern. Use representative data, subgroup evaluation, fairness audits, appropriate feature review and human oversight. |

## Class XII

| Q | Type | Marks | Difficulty | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | MCQ | 1 | Easy | Which learning approach uses labelled training examples? | Supervised learning |
| 2 | MCQ | 1 | Easy | What does a pixel represent? | A small picture element containing image information. |
| 3 | Short | 3 | Medium | Differentiate classification and regression. | Classification predicts categories/classes; regression predicts numerical/continuous values. |
| 4 | Short | 3 | Medium | What is overfitting? | A model learns training-specific patterns too closely and consequently performs poorly on unseen data. |
| 5 | Numerical | 4 | Medium | Given TP=36, TN=44, FP=10, FN=10, calculate accuracy. | (36+44)/(36+44+10+10) = 80%. |
| 6 | Application | 4 | Medium | Explain two practical uses of computer vision. | Examples include image classification, object detection, medical-image analysis, quality inspection or traffic monitoring. |
| 7 | HOTS | 5 | Hard | A medical classifier has high accuracy but many false negatives. Explain why another metric may be more useful. | Accuracy can hide important error types. Recall/sensitivity is important when missing a positive case has high cost. |
| 8 | Case Based | 5 | Hard | A face-recognition attendance system is proposed in a school. Discuss two benefits and three concerns. | Benefits: automation and faster attendance. Concerns: privacy/consent, biometric-data security, demographic bias and misuse/accountability. |

## Assessment notes

- Questions should be practised with exact reasoning, not memorised one-line answers.
- For application/case questions, accept equivalent scientifically correct explanations.
- Numerical answers should show formula and substitution where appropriate.
- Theory questions must remain aligned to ISC AI 883 and must not import unrelated CBSE AI units.
