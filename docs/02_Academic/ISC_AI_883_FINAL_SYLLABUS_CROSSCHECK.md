### XII gaps found and closed

1. **Spam email detection** was added explicitly under Applications of AI, matching the official NLP/ML application example.
2. **Simple linear regression + correlation coefficient** were made explicit under Applications of AI, matching the official statistics/regression coverage.
3. **Official ANN practical tooling** was tightened: NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn preprocessing, Keras/TensorFlow, `fit()`, epochs, batch size and test metrics are represented.
4. **Hyperparameter tuning** was made explicit: grid search/random search and examples including learning rate, batch size, hidden layers and neurons.
5. **Predictive maintenance** was added explicitly under Practical Implications of ML, matching the official syllabus example.
6. The official paradigm examples were made explicit: **handwritten-digit recognition with MLP**, **house-price prediction with a decision tree**, and **image classification with CNN**.
