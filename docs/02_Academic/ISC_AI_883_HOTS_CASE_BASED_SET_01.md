# ISC AI 883 — HOTS & Case-Based Practice Set 01

Board-oriented practice for Examination Year 2027. Theory only; projects/practicals remain separate.

## Class XI

| Q | Type | Marks | Difficulty | Question | Answer / Key points |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Case | 3 | Medium | A school collects attendance, test scores and assignment completion to identify students needing support. Identify the AI problem, features and one ethical concern. | Prediction/classification problem; features are attendance, scores and completion; ethical concern: privacy, bias or misuse. |
| 2 | HOTS | 3 | Medium | A dataset has one extremely large income value. Explain why the median may be preferred to the mean for describing a typical income. | Mean is strongly affected by an extreme value; median is more robust to outliers. |
| 3 | Application | 4 | Hard | A chart shows monthly sales for four products. State two reasons visualization can be useful before modelling and name two suitable chart types. | Reveals trends, comparisons, unusual values/patterns; bar chart for category comparison and line chart for time trend. |
| 4 | Case | 4 | Hard | A linear model predicts exam score from study hours. Explain the roles of independent variable, dependent variable and regression line. | Study hours = independent variable; score = dependent variable; regression line estimates score from study hours. |
| 5 | Ethics | 3 | Medium | An AI system performs well overall but performs poorly for one student group. What should the developer investigate before deployment? | Dataset representation, bias, subgroup performance/error rates and fairness; improve data/model and retest. |

## Class XII

| Q | Type | Marks | Difficulty | Question | Answer / Key points |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Case | 4 | Medium | A learning app recommends questions from previous responses. Explain how machine learning can support this application and why training data matters. | ML learns patterns from historical response data and predicts/recommends suitable items; representative, relevant data improves generalisation. |
| 2 | HOTS | 4 | Hard | Compare neural networks, machine learning and deep learning in terms of relationship and model complexity. | ML is a broad AI learning paradigm; neural networks are ML models inspired by interconnected neurons; deep learning uses neural networks with multiple layers. |
| 3 | ANN | 3 | Medium | A neuron receives inputs with weights and produces an output. Why are weights important? | Weights control the contribution/importance of each input and are adjusted during learning. |
| 4 | ML | 4 | Hard | A model gives 95% accuracy on training data but 68% on unseen data. What does this suggest and what should be checked? | Possible overfitting; check train/test split, data leakage, model complexity and validation performance. |
| 5 | Computer Vision | 4 | Medium | A system receives an image and must decide whether it contains a helmet. Identify the broad CV task and explain one preprocessing step. | Image classification/detection depending on system design; preprocessing may resize, normalise or otherwise standardise image input. |

## Examiner-style reminder

- Read the scenario first and identify the concept being tested.
- For 3–4 mark answers, give distinct points rather than one long paragraph.
- Use syllabus terminology accurately; do not mix ISC AI 883 with CBSE AI 843/402.
