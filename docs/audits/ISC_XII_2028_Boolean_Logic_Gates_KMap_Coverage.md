# ISC Class XII 2028 — Boolean Algebra & Logic Gates Coverage Audit

Source of truth: CISCE ISC Examination Year 2028 Computer Science (868) syllabus.

Official Class XII Section A requires:
- Propositional logic, WFF, truth values and truth tables.
- Satisfiable, unsatisfiable and valid formulae.
- Equivalence laws, De Morgan's laws, implication, biconditional, converse, inverse, contrapositive, chain rule and modus ponens.
- Binary-valued quantities and Boolean operations AND, OR and NOT.
- Boolean laws/theorems including duality, idempotence, commutativity, associativity, distributivity, 0/1 operations, complements, absorption and involution.
- SOP and POS forms.
- Karnaugh maps up to four variables.
- Truth-table verification of Boolean laws.
- Half/full adders and majority circuit.
- Maxterms and minterms.
- Canonical and cardinal representation.
- Reduction using Karnaugh maps and Boolean algebra.
- Elementary gates: NOT, AND, OR, NAND, NOR, XOR, XNOR.
- Gate applications to half/full adders, encoders, decoders and multiplexers.
- NAND and NOR as universal gates and conversion of circuits to NAND-only/NOR-only implementations.

## Completion contract for Target95
Every ISC XII Boolean/Hardware chapter must include, where applicable:
1. Student-friendly theory.
2. Keywords and definitions.
3. Truth tables and worked derivations.
4. Logic-gate diagrams/circuit explanations.
5. SOP/POS, minterm/maxterm and canonical/cardinal worked examples.
6. 2-, 3- and 4-variable K-map examples and practice.
7. Common mistakes and misconceptions.
8. Board-style theory questions.
9. MCQs and output/logic-tracing questions where relevant.
10. Circuit-design and Boolean-simplification practice.
11. PYQ-style questions with marks/difficulty metadata.
12. Revision notes and exam tips.
