## Required evidence for each program

For every row, record:
1. Who authored the problem statement, algorithm explanation, code, sample input and sample output.
2. Whether the item was independently designed or based on a source; if based on a source, record the exact URL/publication, author/publisher, edition/date, access date and permitted license/permission.
3. Whether the wording, order, example values, comments and solution structure closely follow a textbook, guide, website or commercial question bank.
4. What was independently changed or rewritten when a source was used for factual verification.
5. Reviewer and review date.

## Review rules

- Standard programming techniques, language syntax, mathematical facts and algorithmic ideas may be taught accurately; do not claim exclusive authorship over those ideas.
- Do not copy or mechanically paraphrase a source's distinctive explanation, problem wording, worked example, solution presentation or diagram.
- Where provenance is unknown, mark it unresolved and independently rewrite from the syllabus and verified technical facts.
- Passing compilation, runtime tests or syllabus validators is not evidence of copyright clearance.
- Do not mark a row cleared solely because no match was found by a basic text search. Similarity tools are screening aids, not legal determinations.
