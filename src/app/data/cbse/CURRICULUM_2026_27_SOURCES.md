## Rules

1. Subject codes are authoritative identifiers.
2. 083 Computer Science, 065 Informatics Practices, 402 Information Technology and 802 Information Technology are separate curriculum tracks.
3. 083 uses Python as core programming; 065 separately owns Python data-handling libraries such as Pandas and Matplotlib.
4. Do not copy chapter lists between tracks because topics look similar.
5. Do not promote older-session data to 2026–27 without direct verification against the current official source.
6. NCERT textbooks and CBSE support material are secondary mapping sources; official CBSE syllabus remains primary.
