## Data policy

1. Subject codes are authoritative identifiers.
2. 083 Computer Science and 065 Informatics Practices are separate curriculum tracks.
3. Python is core programming for 083; 065 separately tracks Python data-handling libraries such as Pandas and Matplotlib.
4. 402 and 802 are separate Information Technology skill-subject tracks.
5. Do not copy chapter lists between tracks merely because topics look similar.
6. Do not infer chapter/unit names from older sessions.
7. Chapter-level data should be added only after direct verification against the corresponding official 2026-27 source.
8. NCERT textbooks and CBSE support material are secondary mapping sources; the official CBSE syllabus remains the primary curriculum source.
